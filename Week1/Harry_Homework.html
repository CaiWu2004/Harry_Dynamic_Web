Vodka Pasta Recipe

This is a simple recipe for delicious vodka pasta.

Source: Joshua Weissman

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb rigatoni
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 medium shallot, finely chopped
  • 3-4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • Kosher salt
  • 3 Tbsp gochujang
  • 1/2 cup tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp vodka
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1.5 cups heavy cream
  • 1-2 Tbsp unsalted butter, cold and cubed
  • 3/4 cup Percorino Romano, grated and divided
  • Freshly grated Parmigiano reggiano, to taste
  • Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Add the rigatoni and cook according to package instructions until al dente.
  2. In a 5 quart saute pan, add your olive oil, set heat to medium then add the shallot and garlic. Season with a pinch of salt and saute until fragrant and softened, about 1-2 minutes. Stir in the gochujang and tomato paste. Cook, stirring occasionally, for about 2-3 minutes. Cook, stirring often, for 2-3 minutes, or until the mixture begins to thicken, caramelize, and stick to the bottom of the pan.
  3. Deglaze theb sauce with the vodka and water, making sure to scrape up the fond on the bottom of the pan. Then, add the heavy cream, bring it to a boil over medium-high heat, then immediately reduce the heat to low and simmer for 1-2 minutes. Once your sauce is done taste for salt, and add more if needed.
  4. Add your cooked pasta to the sauce pot and toss to combine. Cut of the heat and vigorously stir in your butter and grated pecorino.
  5. Add additional pasta water to aid in the emulsification and coating as needed about 1 tablespoon at a time. Try to avoid adding too much water otherwise you'll end up with a watery pasta sauce. Taste to adjust salt level. This is the last chance to touch up on the flavor
  6. Serve it on a bowl or plate and topped with freshly grated parmigiano Reggiano. Finish off with some cracked black pepper.
